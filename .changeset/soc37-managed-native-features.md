Managed backends (Zernio, PostFast, Post for Me, Postiz, Buffer): native features and provider docs layout.

- Postiz: `cancelScheduled` and `deleteBackendRecord` work again for posts the adapter published or edited. Postiz's public API ignores a `group` sent on create and assigns each post a new one, so the old check refused every post. The adapter now stops sending `group`, reads back the group Postiz assigned, and adds it to the delivery, job, and record identifiers it returns (`<post id>@<time>~<group>`). Cancelling and deleting need such an identifier and still refuse a post that shares its group. Identifiers returned before this release, and ones from `posts.list`, carry no group and are refused; manage those posts in Postiz.
- Buffer: new native reads for channels with the `isLocked` and `product` filters and their timezone, posting goal, link shortening and allowed actions (`listChannels`), organization plan limits and member counts (`listOrganizations`), account timezone and scheduling preferences (`getAccount`), Buffer's experimental per-channel authorization and content rules (`getConfiguration`), and experimental team-member lists for pickers (`listTeamMembers`).
- Buffer: `native.createIdea` now sends media, tags, target services, `aiAssisted`, `templateId`, `cta`, and idea group placement, and the new `native.listIdeaGroups` and `native.listIdeas` read idea groups and ideas filtered by group and tags.
- Buffer adds queue slots, reminder publishing, drafts and approval, tags, ideas, threads, link cards, first comments, video cover frames, Instagram user tags, and per-platform metadata for X, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, Threads, Mastodon, YouTube, TikTok, and Google Business, plus channel reports from `aggregatedPostMetrics` and native methods to edit and reorder posts, read daily posting limits, create ideas, and search Instagram audio. Reminder posts that Buffer marks sent now report `unknown`, and post metrics keep only the `count` and `percentage` units Buffer documents.
- Buffer: publish Substack Notes, list posts with Buffer's status, date, format and tag filters through `native.listPosts`, read back each post's assets, tags, author, allowed actions and failure reason, change approval state and AI-assisted or draft provenance in `native.editPost`, send `aiAssisted` and `draftId` on create, and set image `thumbnailUrl` and `animatedThumbnail`. A video `thumbnail` is now rejected instead of silently dropped, because Buffer rejects it.
- Document Meta's media specifications for Threads and Instagram, with which limits the SDK checks before sending, and note that Zernio currently cannot publish to Reddit, read Reddit messages, or sync Reddit analytics.
- PostFast accounts now keep PostFast's `disabledReason` as `native.disabledReason` on the returned `AccountRecord`, which gains an optional `native` field for provider-specific account details. Connection states are unchanged: `CONNECTED` is `connected`, `DISABLED` is `reconnect-required`, and anything else is `unknown`.
- Zernio: `zernio(options)` and `zernioNative(options)` built from the same options object share one client and default media store again, so media refs uploaded through the normalized adapter work in native submissions. PostFast: `setInboxStatus` and `assignInboxConversation` report `ambiguous_outcome` when a dispatched write returns a body that is not a Conversation.
- Post for Me previews send and read media objects, PostFast X reposts and Zernio LinkedIn reshares no longer require text, and Zernio `validatePost` keeps the `error` and `warning` explanations.
- Post for Me lists TikTok Business accounts as TikTok, maps followers and friends TikTok privacy, photo titles and music, media alt text and video thumbnails, X polls, quotes and communities, Instagram and Facebook placement, collaborators and locations, trial Reels, YouTube tags, categories and disclosures, LinkedIn documents and reshares, multiple Pinterest boards and Instagram saves and watch time, and adds native connection platform data and re-auth, disconnect, delete, post updates, previews and webhook management. Post for Me no longer rejects `altText` or `FOLLOWER_OF_CREATOR` and `MUTUAL_FOLLOW_FRIENDS` TikTok privacy.
- Post for Me: `mediaOptions[].skipProcessing` maps to `skip_processing`, `native.previewPosts` previews several accounts with per-account `account_configurations`, and `native.findFeedPosts` filters the feed by `social_post_id`, `external_post_id` or `platform_post_id`. `previewPost` now sends the same platform options a publish sends.
- Post for Me: `mediaOptions[].tags` sends Instagram and Facebook media tags as Post for Me's `UserTagDto`, with `x` and `y` passed through unchanged because Post for Me does not state their scale. A Threads target's `placement` (`reels` or `timeline`) is sent unchanged as `platform_configurations.threads.placement`.
- PostFast: Facebook link carousels take `carouselEndCard`, Social Inbox conversations filter by `statuses` and `assignedToUserId`, and inbox items take an `order`.
- PostFast's native adapter can now triage Social Inbox conversations. `setInboxStatus({ conversationId, status })` sets `OPEN`, `SNOOZED` or `CLOSED`, and `assignInboxConversation({ conversationId, assigneeUserId })` assigns a conversation to a workspace member, or unassigns it when `assigneeUserId` is `null`. Both return the updated conversation and throw `ambiguous_outcome` when PostFast's response does not show the requested change.
- PostFast maps first comments, YouTube tags, playlists, synthetic-media disclosure, thumbnails, language and captions, TikTok titles, music and covers, Instagram Stories, Reels, collaborators, locations, AI labels and trial Reels, Facebook Reels, Stories, collaborators, places, target countries and link carousels, X reposts, Threads topics, LinkedIn documents and Google Business topics, calls to action, events and offers; allows Bluesky MP4 video; adds `posts.list`, follower counts, Reel skip rate and TikTok favorites; and adds native drafts, approval, bulk delete, caption upload, follower history, lookups and Social Inbox helpers.
- Postiz: publish to Slack, Lemmy, Warpcast, Nostr, VK, Kick, Medium, Dev.to, Hashnode, WordPress, Listmonk, Dribbble, Tumblr, MeWe, Moltbook, Skool and Whop channels with their Postiz settings, send Twitch announcements, repeat posts with `repeatEveryDays`, edit posts with `native.editPost`, list posts by customer with `native.listPosts`, and report Postiz's failure reason on failed deliveries. These channels are now listed by default under their Postiz identifier instead of being skipped.
- Postiz adds post listing, channel analytics, a customer `group` filter, adjusted uploads, more OAuth connect providers, and native methods for the rest of its public API (workspace, channel settings and tools, free slots, media library, notifications, post status and settings, missing release IDs, AI video, and clipping).
- Postiz maps drafts, workspace tags, short links, thread parts, video cover frames, and more platform settings: X communities, articles and disclosure labels, Instagram stories, trial reels, collaborators and reel audio, Facebook links, stories, text backgrounds and video titles, YouTube thumbnails and tags, TikTok titles on direct posts and photo music, LinkedIn carousels, Pinterest colors, Reddit link posts, and Google Business post types.
- Buffer's native `listPosts` now rejects the `carousel` and `thread` `postTypes` filters locally, as Buffer does, because no stored post format resolves to them. Use `post` for formats Buffer does not store separately.
- Zernio: native `listPosts` with `source: "external"` now pages by number and returns the next page as `nextCursor`, so synced history past the first page is reachable. OAuth app records keep the documented `webhook` setup for Facebook and Instagram apps and `slackEvents` and `hasSlackSigningSecret` for Slack apps. Preparation rejects `recycling` on YouTube and TikTok, which Zernio excludes from recycling.
- Zernio: destination, sound and location lookups, Instagram publishing limit, account groups, per-account settings, your own OAuth apps and welcome screen, Discord channel connect, reconnect and branding options on `createConnection`, usage stats, activity logs and filtered `listPosts`.
- Zernio: post timelines, the analytics change feed and dashboard, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and Google Business analytics extras, inbox analytics, Reddit comment edits and YouTube comment moderation status, conversation search, archive, delete, accept, handover and labels, and Messenger, Instagram and Telegram bot settings.
- `zernioNative` adds `googleBusinessPerformance({ account, metrics?, fromDate?, toDate? })`, which reads a Google Business Profile location's daily performance metrics from Zernio's `GET /v1/analytics/googlebusiness/performance`: Maps and Search impressions by device, website and call clicks, direction requests, conversations, bookings and food orders. Metric names and `YYYY-MM-DD` dates are checked before the request, and each returned metric comes back as a `total` with daily values. A response for a different account is rejected.
- Zernio adds recent X search, Reddit and TikTok comments, Reddit and Telegram messages, native comment replies, hide, delete, private replies, likes and pins, X reposts, bookmarks and follows, conversation read marks, new conversations and attachments, mentions and reviews, webhook management, follower history, account insights, YouTube daily views, best posting times and content decay, and decodes message-state, reaction, review, cancelled and recycled webhook events.
- Zernio's native adapter adds Bluesky app-password connect, profile create, update and delete, account moves, health and disconnect, drafts, approval and queue submission with review, reschedule, published-post edits, YouTube metadata updates, retry, dry-run validation, TikTok creator info and live platform post reads.
- Zernio's native helpers moved to their own subpath, `@opencoredev/social-sdk/cloud/zernio-native`, so an app that only publishes through `zernio()` no longer bundles them. `zernioNative(options)` takes the same options as `zernio(options)` and sends the same requests. The normalized `zernio()` adapter is unchanged apart from dropping its `native` property.

  Migration: replace `zernio(options).native.method(input, context)` and `social.native(backend, { acknowledgeUnsafe: true }).method(input, context)` on a Zernio backend with `zernioNative(options).method(input, context)`, imported from `@opencoredev/social-sdk/cloud/zernio-native`. Native-only types such as `ZernioConnectionOptions`, `ZernioSubmitPostInput`, `ZernioWebhookInput` and `ZernioBotSettingInput` are now imported from that subpath too. Pass the same options object to `zernio` and `zernioNative` (or a shared `mediaStore`) so media refs uploaded through the normalized adapter remain usable in native submissions. Native calls still skip the client's authorization policy, so keep running your own tenant checks before them.

- Zernio: per-attachment `mediaOptions` subtitles and video thumbnails, evergreen `recycling`, X `longVideo` and `mediaTaggedUsers`, Facebook `pageId` with `listFacebookPages` and `selectFacebookPage`, Pinterest board sections and video covers, YouTube `notifySubscribers`, Google Business event and offer posts, Telegram, Reddit and Discord post options, and `validatePosts` with per-destination `customContent` and `customMedia`.
- Zernio maps native publish options (X quotes, polls, labels and threads, Threads topic tags, Bluesky languages, YouTube tags and playlists, TikTok covers and music, Instagram Stories, Reels, collaborators and tags, Facebook Reels, Stories and link carousels, LinkedIn documents, reposts and polls, and first comments), unpublishes from Pinterest, Reddit, Discord, Telegram and Google Business Profile, and deletes failed or cancelled records.
- Zernio: Instagram, Facebook and YouTube read extras, on-demand external post sync, CSV bulk upload, length, media and subreddit validators, Google Business reviews and listing management, Reddit votes, search, feeds and comments, Discord DMs and server tools, and decoding for account permission, rate-limit, conversation, referral, message-played and analytics-synced webhooks.
- Zernio Instagram targets accept `audioConfiguration` and `muteAudio`. `audioConfiguration` attaches a catalog track to a Reel by `audioId`, with optional `audioVolume` and `videoVolume` integers from 0 to 100; preparation rejects it outside a Reel or with an invalid ID or volume. `muteAudio` asks Zernio to strip the sound from the video before publishing, which cannot be undone on Instagram; preparation rejects it on posts without a video. Previously both keys failed preparation as `options.unmapped`.
- Zernio `deleteProfile`, Post for Me `deleteAccount`, Buffer `createIdea`, and PostFast `replyToInboxItem`, `privateReply` and `moderateInboxItem` throw `ambiguous_outcome` with `reconcile-first` when a dispatched write returns a body that cannot confirm it; PostFast replies require the created `OUTBOUND` item's `id`.
- Buffer keeps Instagram `userTags` on an image whose `altText` is empty, sending `altText: ""` with them.
- Zernio native `listPosts` has no `updatedSince` filter, because Zernio's list-posts reference does not list it and answers 400; it now refuses the key, and `analyticsDelta` syncs changed posts.
