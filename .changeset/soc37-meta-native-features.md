Facebook, Instagram and Threads: native features, Graph API v27.0.

- The Instagram and Facebook adapters now call Meta Graph API v27.0. Both move from v25.0, and so does the Facebook Login dialog URL. `instagramOAuth` account discovery defaults to v27.0, and `instagramApiVersion` still overrides it.

  Facebook `native.getReactionCounts` sends one `GET /{object-id}/reactions?type=TYPE&limit=0&summary=total_count` request per reaction type and no longer uses field aliases (`.as()`). Meta closed aliases to new apps on 4 November 2026 and removes them in v28.0. Counting all six types now takes six requests instead of one.

  Facebook `native.updatePost` accepts `isAiGenerated`, which sends v27.0's `is_ai_generated` to add or remove the AI-generated label on an existing post.

- Facebook Pages adds native post Insights and Page Insights for any period, `date_preset`, or 90-day range, and Facebook webhooks now name feed, mention, and ratings changes as `field.item.verb` (for example `feed.reaction.add`), report shared-post removals as `post.removed`, decode proposed Page changes as `account.updated`, and name Messenger reads, deliveries, reactions, and postbacks.

  Migration: Facebook `feed` events now carry `originalType` values such as `feed.comment.add` instead of `feed`; match on the `feed.` prefix where you compared against `feed`.

- Facebook: Login helpers (dialog URL, code and long-lived token exchange, debug_token inspection, /me/permissions), an `appSecret` option that adds appsecret_proof to every Graph call, and an `onRateLimitUsage` callback for X-App-Usage, X-Page-Usage and X-Business-Use-Case-Usage headers.
- Facebook Messenger: Messenger profile, handover protocol, custom labels, utility templates and call settings, plus decoding of standby, handover, message edit, policy enforcement, account linking and feedback webhooks (account linking codes are redacted).
- Facebook Messenger: quick replies, reply_to, notification_type, metadata and persona_id on sends, plus sender actions, personas, reusable attachment upload, User Profile lookup, conversation filters and full message details.
- The Facebook Pages adapter now implements Messenger: `messages.listConversations`, `messages.listMessages`, and `messages.send` for Pages, plus native `sendMessage` with `messaging_type`, the `HUMAN_AGENT` tag, and image, video, audio, or file attachments, and native `privateReply` for comments.
- Facebook Pages: unblock and list blocked people, unsubscribe the app, like or unlike as the Page, newest-first comments with private-reply and attachment fields, reshares, assigned business users, location Pages, video playlists, and profile picture and cover photo updates.
- Facebook Pages adds native Page detail, settings, and role management, proposed-change responses, ratings and reviews, Pages Search, person blocking, webhook app subscription, comment reads with `filter` and summaries, comments with image or GIF attachments, comment edits, reaction counts by type, and a `facebookMention` helper for `@[id]` mentions.
- Facebook Pages publishing accepts native options for place and user tags, photo tags, `no_story`, backdating, audience and feed targeting, call-to-action buttons, link carousels, feelings, and video title, visibility, category, tag, and label fields; uploads custom video thumbnails, large Blob videos in chunks, and Reels from bytes; saves Reel drafts with title, place, and AI disclosure; adds native crossposting, Reel collaborator invitations, and copyright checks; enforces Meta's 30 Reels per 24 hours per adapter; and schedules Page videos up to 6 months and Reels up to 29 days ahead.

  Migration: Reels scheduled 29 to 30 days ahead are now rejected at preparation, and a Facebook video `thumbnail` must be a `Blob` instead of being rejected outright.

- Facebook Pages adds native photo and video Stories, Story listing, live video creation and ending, scheduled, tagged, and visitor post listings, Reel and uploaded-photo listings, and post edits that change the message or pin or hide the post; post reads now include `full_picture`.
- Facebook video and live: branded content, album uploads, 360 photo and video, multilingual descriptions, video replacement and expiration publish options, plus video thumbnails, SRT captions, albums, scheduled and configurable live broadcasts with stream health, live polls, and video and Reels Insights.
- Instagram adds native comment creation, single-comment reads, ordered comments, mention replies and private replies, plus Instagram Login rich messaging: images and attachment IDs, files, the heart sticker, shared posts, replies to a message, quick replies, generic and button templates, the HUMAN_AGENT tag, reactions, sender actions, ice breakers, the persistent menu, and the User Profile API.
- Instagram adds Facebook Login `like` and `unlike`, `getPollResults` and `listPromptResponses` for caption add-ons, `postedAfter` and `postedBefore` on the tags readers, media audio, Reel feed, comment-state and total-view fields, own-profile `has_profile_pic` and `is_published`, and `issues` warnings on a `published` outcome when `media_publish` drops the caption, user tags, poll or prompt.
- Instagram adds native media and account insights (including `views`, `total_interactions`, breakdowns and audience demographics), webhook subscription, Facebook Page account discovery, and Facebook Login product tagging; normalized post metrics now also request `views` and `total_interactions`. Instagram webhooks now decode Facebook Login `mentions` as `mention.received`, `story_insights` as `insights.updated`, and messaging postbacks, reactions, seen receipts, referrals and edits as `message.postback`, `message.reaction`, `message.read`, `message.referral` and `message.edited`. Migration: these deliveries previously decoded as `unknown`, and `SocialEvent["type"]` has new members, so exhaustive switches over it need the new cases.
- Instagram publishing accepts carousels of up to 20 items, checks the 30-hashtag and 20-mention caption limits, adds typed container options (Reel cover, thumbnail offset, audio name, trial Reels, collaborators, user tags, location, AI disclosure, and Facebook Login partnership, poll, comment prompt, product tag and audio fields), and adds native audio search and copyright check reads; unknown or misplaced Instagram options are now rejected before any request.
- Instagram adds Facebook Login resumable video uploads, richer post fields (media URLs, like and comment counts, and owner counters), and native reads for carousel children, Stories, live media, collaborations and invites, the connected Threads user, oEmbed, upcoming events, and recently searched hashtags.
- Instagram messaging now works with the `facebook-login` flavor through `graph.facebook.com/{ig-user-id}` for Unified Login system user tokens, so `messages.read` and `messages.write` are available on both flavors. New `findConversation` (`user_id` lookup), Unified Login `getClientBusinessId` and `listAssignedAccounts`, Page-less `subscribeWebhooks`, `instagramOAuth` `forceReauth` and `enableFacebookLogin` options, and typed webhook variants for deleted, self-test, unsupported, handoff and story-reply messages plus `messaging_policy_enforcement` and `response_feedback`. Migration: Facebook Login messaging calls that used to throw `unsupported_capability` now send requests, and `subscribeWebhooks` with Instagram Login now rejects a `pageId`.
- Meta native writes that lose their answer or return no ID after dispatch reject with `ambiguous_outcome` and `reconcile-first`, and Instagram `uploadVideo` shares the operation context's elapsed budget. Instagram and Threads native writes, and Facebook publishing and comment replies, now also treat an HTTP 200 answer that is not the documented object, ID or `success: true` as `ambiguous_outcome` instead of a success, a raw validation error or a definite failure.
- Instagram: `native.searchAudio` accepts Meta's documented `audio` response array, and `native.getAudio` keeps `cover_artwork_thumbnail_url`. Facebook: `native.listReelCollaborators` accepts the single collaborator status object Meta's Reels guide documents.
- Instagram keeps the `config_issue` and `caption_add_on_attachment_status` warnings from `media_publish` in its workflow, so `posts.getDelivery` and `native.resumePublication` return the same `issues` after publication. Facebook album photo uploads return a post reference with `native.objectType: "photo"` and a `photo:{id}` delivery; `posts.get`, `getDelivery`, and post metrics read it with Photo fields instead of failing on Page-post fields.
- Threads: add the `autoPublishText` publish option, which sends `auto_publish_text` to publish a text-only post on container creation. Because Meta does not document which ID that call returns, the outcome is `unknown` with `backendState: "AUTO_PUBLISHED"` and the SDK never calls `threads_publish` for it.
- Threads gains native list reads with date ranges and cursors, full media fields, poll results, own replies, ghost posts, public profile posts, recent searches, geo-gating eligibility, location search, ranged user insights with per-link clicks, follower demographics, `debugToken` and `oembed`; mention and publish webhooks now decode as `mention.received` and `post.published`, and `comments.write` declares the `threads_manage_replies` scope Meta requires for replies.

  Migration: Threads `mentions` and `publish` webhook events used to arrive as `unknown`. Handlers that matched them by `originalType` on `unknown` events should match `mention.received` and `post.published` instead, and exhaustive switches over `SocialEvent["type"]` need the two new members.

- Threads publishing accepts new `ThreadsPublishOptions` for reply audiences, reply approvals, topic tags, quotes, polls, GIPHY GIFs, AI labels, spoilers, text attachments, ghost posts, location tags, geo-gating and Instagram Story cross-sharing, and `prepareTarget` rejects posts with more than five unique links.
- Threads: decode profile lookup follower and 7-day engagement counts with native `lookupProfile`, accept `fields` on native search and `fields` and `before` on conversation and pending-reply reads, and add `threadsPostIntentUrl` and `threadsFollowIntentUrl` web intent builders.
